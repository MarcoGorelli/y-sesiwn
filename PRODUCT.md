# Product

## Platform

web

## Users
No single primary audience; the product serves these equally:

- **Session players** (fiddle, whistle, flute, harp, guitar, accordion…) learning tunes before a *sesiwn* and pulling them up at one, often on a phone, sometimes without signal.
- **Learners and beginners** new to Welsh traditional music, learning an instrument or their first tunes.
- **Teachers, tutors and groups** sharing tunes, keys and sets with students by link or QR code.
- **The curious**: anyone interested in Welsh traditional music and culture, including English speakers who want to know how to say a tune's name.

## Product Purpose
Y Sesiwn (https://ysesiwn.cymru/) is a free, open-source collection of Welsh folk tunes to learn, play and share. Every tune has sheet music, playback at any tempo, a change to any key, and its own link to send to a friend before a session. Success is someone finding the tune they need (by name, by type and key, or by playing its first notes), learning it in their own key and speed, and arriving at a session ready to play.

## Positioning
- **Welsh-specific:** a curated Welsh repertoire with credits as printed on the original scores (Welsh credit words glossed), versions of the same tune grouped together, pronunciation guides for Welsh names, and a directory of sessions in Wales.
- **Bilingual, at parity:** Welsh and English are equal; neither is a translation afterthought.
- **Learning tools:** change key and tempo, loop a part and speed it up, count-in and click, tablature, suggested chords, and find a tune by playing its first notes to the microphone.
- **Free, offline, no tracking:** static, open source, no adverts, nothing sold; works offline after the first visit and can be installed to a phone's home screen.

## Operating Context
- Used at home to learn and practise, and at a sesiwn (pub, festival) on a phone, often with poor or no signal.
- Tunes are shared as links (carrying the chosen key), QR codes, and printed sheet music.
- Contributors add tunes and corrections by GitHub pull request; problems are reported via a pre-filled GitHub issue from each tune page.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- **Stack (existing):** static site, plain HTML/CSS/JS in `site/`, built by `build_site.py` (Python stdlib only) into `_site/`, deployed to GitHub Pages via `.github/workflows/pages.yml` only if tests pass. Relative URLs so it also works under a sub-path.
- **ABC is the single source of truth:** everything shown (score, playback, key changes, details, chords, melody search) is generated from each `tunes/<folder>/tune.abc`, rendered with abcjs. No MIDI or score images used by the site.
- **Self-contained:** all assets (abcjs, soundfonts, fonts, icons) are in the repo; the only third-party contact is cookieless GoatCounter analytics on the live site. Service worker for offline use.
- **Bilingual text:** site strings via `tr("English", "Cymraeg")` in `site/app.js` and `data-cy` in `site/index.html`; Welsh pages in `site/*.cy.md`. Any copy change must be made in both languages.
- **Surfaces:** home, search by name, find by notes, browse by type and key, tune pages with versions, full-screen practice mode, sets, sessions directory with map and calendar, About, offline and contribution guides.
- **Tested:** pytest + Playwright cover every tune rendering, phone widths (no horizontal scroll), offline, microphone search, printing, and axe accessibility on several pages in light and dark at desktop and phone widths. Any JS error fails the tests.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: **Y Sesiwn**. Domain: ysesiwn.cymru.
- Educational and non-commercial: nothing sold, no adverts.
- Credit composers and arrangers as printed on the original scores; honour requests to change credits or take a tune down.
- Instruments drawn for the project (triple harp, crwth, pibgorn, lovespoon) and a Welsh quilt/*carthen* motif are existing identity assets in `static/` and `site/` (generated by scripts in `design/`).
- Voice: plain, friendly, short; Welsh and English at equal standing. Thanks expressed in Welsh (*Diolch yn fawr*).

## Evidence on Hand
- 722 tunes (952 versions, in 978 folders in `tunes/`), from CLERA's Alawon Cymru (arrangements mostly by Meurig Williams) and Brian Martin's ABC transcriptions (Blodau'r Grug, Edward Jones, Mary Richards and others), credited per tune.
- Sessions and places in `sessions.json` / `places.json`; pronunciations in `pronunciation.json`.
- No testimonials, user counts, press or endorsements exist; do not invent them. Organisations linked on the About page (CLERA, trac cymru, Tŷ Cerdd) are pointers, not endorsements.

## Product Principles
1. **Get to the tune fast.** Every path (name, notes, type and key, a shared link) should land on playable music with as few steps as possible.
2. **Works at the session.** Phone-first, offline, readable at a glance, nothing that needs a signal or an account.
3. **Two languages, one product.** Welsh and English are equals in every string, page and decision.
4. **The ABC is the truth.** Derive everything from the tune files; never hand-maintain a second copy.
5. **Free and respectful.** No ads, no tracking beyond cookieless counts, proper credit for the people whose music this is.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Axe checks must pass on key pages in light and dark mode, desktop and phone widths.
- Tune names carry `lang` (Welsh or English) so screen readers pronounce them correctly; focus moves to the heading on page change; search announces result counts.
- Selections remain visible in Windows high-contrast mode; full keyboard access with shortcuts (`/`, space, `?`).
- Respect reduced motion (cross-fades can be disabled).
